# About page — internal editorial notes

The site's established purpose is independent education. The public About page
therefore presents the eight supplied names and their profile URLs only, as
non-endorsing references. It does not offer treatment, consultation booking or
clinical services.

The original supplied promotional material has not been deleted. It remains in
`build/quantum-health.js` for editorial review, including the doctor role and
specialty descriptions, qualifications, experience claims, Dr. Sunitha
Devaraj's supplied leadership and wellness copy, service descriptions,
achievements, and the Quantum Health Stemcell Resort promotion. These details
are not confirmed facts and must not be restored to the public page without
source verification and approval. The home-page promotional panel is also
excluded from generated output to keep it consistent with the site's purpose.

No approved doctor photographs were found in the source assets; doctor records
currently have `portrait: null`. The About page uses abstract monogram
placeholders rather than generated or unrelated portraits.
