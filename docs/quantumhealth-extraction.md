## Page 5
**Personalised care. Informed choices. Individualised planning.**

Breast enhancement is a personal decision. The right approach depends on your anatomy, health, existing breast tissue, aesthetic preferences, and expectations.

At Quantum Health, our approach centres on understanding these factors before discussing available options. We aim to make complex treatment choices easier to understand by presenting the potential benefits, limitations, recovery considerations, and risks of each suitable approach.

Whether you are exploring breast augmentation surgery, fat transfer, or non-surgical treatments, our focus is on transparent information and a carefully considered treatment plan.

**Our principles**
- Individual assessment: Recommendations based on medical history, anatomy and personal goals.
- Safety-first planning: Clear discussion of contraindications, alternatives and potential complications.
- Evidence-informed care: Treatment explanations grounded in available evidence and applicable regulations.
- Respect and discretion: A confidential, respectful environment for discussing sensitive concerns.

**Section 3: Explore our treatment pathways**
- Non-surgical options: Explore selected injectable, regenerative and skin-quality approaches, with particular attention to evidence, suitability and approved indications. → Explore non-surgical treatments
- Surgical options: Understand breast implants, silicone gel-filled implants, and autologous fat transfer, including recovery and long-term considerations. → Explore surgical treatments

**Important clinical distinction:** injectable fillers, threads, PRP and biostimulators should not be presented as interchangeable alternatives to breast augmentation surgery. Their evidence, indications and ability to change breast volume differ substantially. The FDA specifically advises against dermal filler injections for breast enlargement. *(FDA +1)*
